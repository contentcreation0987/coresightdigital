Privacy

What we do with your information

Short version: we collect only what you send us, we use it to reply to you, and we do not sell it.

What we collect

The name, WhatsApp number and brief you submit through the contact form or send us on WhatsApp, Instagram or email. If you browse the site, our analytics records anonymous usage data — pages viewed, approximate city, device type.

Why we collect it

To answer your enquiry, quote work, and — if you become a client — to run the services you have asked for. We also use aggregate analytics to see which pages are useful and which are not.

Who we share it with

Nobody, except the platforms we need to operate: our email provider, our hosting provider, and the advertising and analytics platforms named on this site (Meta, Google) where you have interacted with our ads. We do not sell or rent your details.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for two years, then deleted. Client records are kept as long as the engagement runs, plus whatever period Pakistani tax and accounting rules require.

Your choices

Write to hello@coresightdigital.com and ask us to send you a copy of what we hold, correct it, or delete it. We will action it within 30 days.

Cookies

This site sets analytics and advertising cookies. You can block them in your browser; the site works fine without them.
